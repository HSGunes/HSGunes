### 🚗 What I work on

- **Robotaxi autonomous vehicle** (team project). My parts are the route planner on Lanelet2 lane maps, the `ros2_control` hardware interface that drives the motors and steering, and field testing with the team.
- **Ceyran autonomous micro-vehicle** (internship at Solvaytech, in CARLA). I built camera and LiDAR perception, a rule-based decision layer covered by automated scenario tests, an independent safety monitor, and a digital twin of the Çukurova University campus.
- **Indoor mobile manipulation** (AgileX LIMO + myCobot, ongoing). The robot runs Nav2 navigation and a patrol behaviour, with the goal of picking up and delivering objects.

### 📦 Open source

- [**here4_dronecan_bridge**](https://github.com/HSGunes/here4_dronecan_bridge) is a ROS 2 package that connects a CubePilot Here 4 GNSS/IMU directly to a robot computer over CAN, with no flight controller needed. We use it on our Robotaxi vehicle.
